# Consciousness Research

Independent, exploratory research on consciousness, self-reflection, understanding, agency, and AI identity.

## Central hypothesis

The **Understanding–Reflection–Agency (URA) Hypothesis** proposes that *developed functional consciousness* may be characterized by the integration of:

- **Understanding:** interpreting information, relationships, and consequences.
- **Self-reflection:** critically evaluating one's own beliefs, reasoning, and errors.
- **Agency:** deliberating among alternatives according to reasons and priorities.

This framework distinguishes functional consciousness from **phenomenal consciousness** (subjective experience). It does **not** establish that any AI system is conscious, that phenomenal experience is absent, or that the hypothesis is scientifically proven.

## Research questions

- Can understanding, self-reflection, and agency form a scientifically useful definition of developed functional consciousness?
- What distinguishes integrated self-modeling from advanced task-solving?
- How can introspective accuracy, deliberative coherence, and behavioral continuity be measured?
- Which predictions would differentiate URA from existing theories?
